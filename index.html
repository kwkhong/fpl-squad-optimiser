    03 / Method

    Strong picks.
    Visible logic.

    The model targets the next actionable deadline, estimates expected minutes and availability, shrinks noisy xG/xA and bonus rates, derives fixture scoring from a recency-weighted Poisson team model, and prices uncertainty separately from expected points. A deterministic beam search then maximises the legal starting XI, captain and weighted bench while preventing next-gameweek goalkeeper/defender versus opposing attacker clashes whenever a clash-free legal XI exists—not an arbitrary sum of all 15 players.

    Official FPL data and recent gameweek histories refresh automatically. Transfer mode deducts four-point hits beyond the selected free transfers. Confirm late team news before each deadline.
